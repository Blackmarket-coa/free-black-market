# BMC monthly revenue targets and launch priorities

**Updated:** November 21, 2026  
**Status:** Extension of the conditional 2027 analysis; no new price, contract, release date, app feature or spending is authorized.  
**Companions:** [Ecosystem forecast](BMC_ECOSYSTEM_FINANCIAL_ANALYSIS.md) · [FBM monetization](FBM_MONETIZATION_PLAN.md) · [Shared game/creator plan](FBM_COALITION_GAME_AND_CREATOR_COMMERCE_PLAN.md)

## 1. What the deeper analysis changes

The annual scenarios remain $1,461 downside / $17,318 base / $125,802 upside in BMC revenue. The next decision is which supported offers can produce that income at sustainable cost.

Under the base assumptions, founder-owned products contribute about **$6,044** after their direct payment/distribution costs, before shared operations, unpaid founder labor and tax. Third-party creator game packs produce only **$74** in annual BMC commission and about **$66** in incremental contribution in the constant-audience model, before any separately incurred game-development, maintenance or moderation cost. Ordinary third-party downloads produce about $308 in commission and $272 in incremental contribution on the same basis.

Those game-pack amounts exclude BMC-owned game products, which would fall within the first-party product pool. They also exclude any game-driven acquisition or retention effect: no measured uplift exists, and adding one to the forecast would double count benefits already assumed in active-member growth or product conversion. Creator earnings and community benefit can be meaningful even when BMC's direct fee is small.

Preserve the encounter-first game plan. Larger raids, AR and extensive custom tooling need evidence of usefulness, operating capacity and funding beyond the direct third-party game fee. This is a sequencing decision; core participation, discoveries, aid and reputation do not become purchase requirements.

The default branches of all three repos were checked again during this extension and had no newer commits than the original financial review. Additional Blackmask security/device-discovery ideas remain unpriced research rather than a new forecast revenue stream.

## 2. Unit economics

These are proposed average prices, not a public price list. Base costs use 3% assumed refunds, 75% web-card/25% illustrative 15%-fee native digital billing, and the prior documented processing costs. Storage adds a $0.75/payer monthly direct cost allowance.

| Offer | Candidate price | BMC revenue after refunds | Contribution after direct costs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| FBM enhancement | $25.00 | $24.25 | $22.54 |
| Blackout enhancement | $15.00 | $14.55 | $13.44 |
| Blackmask enhancement | $12.00 | $11.64 | $10.70 |
| Blackmask storage | $4.00/month | $3.88 | $2.67 |

Contribution excludes common infrastructure, general support/moderation, product maintenance, unpaid founder labor and tax. A creator collaboration with royalties requires an additional agreed cost; current first-party modeling uses founder ownership. A free, earned, restored or already-owned copy is not a new paid unit.

At these assumptions, selling a $25 BMC-owned product contributes about $22.54 before common costs. A $25 third-party sale earns only a fraction of its price as commission. Do not use full creator sale prices to justify BMC's operating budget.

Approximately **745 actives** cover the $250 fixed baseline. These counts are not signups or social followers, and surplus is before founder pay and tax.

The larger targets exceed the founder's $1,000 monthly spending capacity and eventually require an active population larger than the current follower count. They require retained operating receipts, validated capacity and staffing/cost planning. Seller payouts and restricted community funds cannot fund BMC operations.

## 6. Focused launch catalogue

Start with a small catalogue that can be delivered and supported. The names and prices below are experiments, not claims of implemented products. Confirm source rights, capability, license scope, permissions, supported devices and restoration first.

| Candidate | App | Candidate price | Required evidence before selling |
|---|---|---:|---|
| One useful stall/producer tool or portal configuration | FBM | $25 one-time | A working capability beyond the free starter; account/organization ownership; one-time checkout, restore and refund |
| One original visual, sound or creator asset pack | Blackout | $15 one-time | Actual original files, preview, signed delivery and supported use; selling files alone does not prove companion/game installation |
| One original mask, sticker or community-branding pack | Blackmask | $12 one-time | Correct rendering in supported clients, free-client readability, rights, restore and equivalent earned ownership |
| Additional encrypted hosted storage | Blackmask | $4/month candidate | Defined quota, actual unit costs, billing, cancellation, export and restore; no undefined unlimited promise |
| Finite setup/training work | Relevant app | Quote per delivered scope | Named accountable provider, scope/acceptance, actual payment and cost; community-provider earnings remain theirs |

Use the real tool/asset catalogue to select the offers. Do not manufacture three separate products solely to hit the allocation percentages. A cross-app bundle can be tested later with credit for already-owned items; its receipt replaces the included individual sales rather than adding to them.

Security fixes, stronger encryption, essential recovery, basic participation and accessibility remain free core. New privacy/security OSS can support optional organization, configuration or local convenience tools once tested, but a project license does not establish zero integration/maintenance cost or a sellable native feature. Hidden-device detection has no approved price or revenue in this model.

## 7. First 90 days after a verified public launch

The clock begins with a working launch, rather than assuming an app-store approval date. These are measurement periods, not release promises.

| Period | Commercial objective | Evidence and decision |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Validate one first-party offer and one ordinary local exchange with actual participating groups | Record unique paying customers, delivered units, real receipts, refunds, costs and support time. Do not count tests, earned copies or failed payments as sales. |
| Days 31–60 | Establish repeat useful commerce and one complete creator-download path | Measure repeat customers, local supply/matching, real uploaded bytes, reopening and settled creator earnings. Remove broken or misleading offers. |
| Days 61–90 | Validate a cooperative game activity and evaluate one original supported paid pack | Compare game participants with appropriately matched nonparticipants; describe observational differences without claiming causal lift. Verify pack installation, rights, refund and settlement before sales. |
| After each period | Replace assumptions and constrain scope | Use actual cohorts, direct cost invoices, product availability and work capacity; revise growth, conversion and margins before expanding paid acquisition or costly features. |

The suggested first operating milestone is to cover actual costs with repeatable delivered sales, followed by $1,000/month BMC revenue. A one-time launch spike does not establish either milestone as repeatable.

## 8. Acquisition and spending discipline

The audience provides an initial owned distribution channel, not a guaranteed conversion rate. First measure costs of successful delivery and actual retained contribution. Do not assign a speculative lifetime value or spend the entire $22.54 first-product contribution to acquire a customer; it also needs to fund common costs, maintenance and founder labor.

Use attribution across Blackout, FBM and Blackmask without triple counting people or sales. Ask whether gameplay brings additional unique participants, repeated exchanges or paid first-party demand. Do not monetize precise movement histories, vault content or private conversation analysis to obtain that measurement.

Do not spend toward the $1,000 ceiling merely because it is available. Release costly capabilities within measured resource limits and leave room for failed payments, refunds, repairs and provider variation. Uncommitted grants, merchandise, paid community roles, device-discovery research and item transfers remain excluded from forecast income.
